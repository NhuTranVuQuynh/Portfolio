Swedish design, Belgian hands

Many Belgians drive a Volvo without knowing it was built down the road

Volvo Car Gent opened in November 1965 as the first Volvo factory outside Sweden. Today it builds the XC40, EX40, EC40, V60 and, since April 2025, the EX30.
