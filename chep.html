App concept for CHEP

No truck drives home empty.

Return Match finds empty pallets that are waiting along a truck's way back, so the return trip collects them instead of running empty.

Example

  1. 1A truck leaves Cologne and delivers 300 pallets to Coca-Cola in Antwerp.
  2. 2On its way back to Germany it passes Tilburg, where 278 empty pallets are waiting at Albert Heijn.
  3. 3The app matches the two. The truck collects the pallets and no second truck is needed.

Try it: type shipment 94839893 (match found) or 94839894 (no match yet) into the app and press Go. Any other 8-digit number shows a different shipment.

Concept and prototype by Jessica (Nhu Tran). Not an official CHEP product. Customer names, routes and figures are demo data.

How it works

  1. 1A truck delivers full pallets to a customer.
  2. 2The network looks for empty pallets waiting close to its way back.
  3. 3The driver gets one extra stop and collects them, instead of driving home empty.
