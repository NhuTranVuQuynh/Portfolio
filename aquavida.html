Technology

How the Soundsonic bottle works

A speaker built into the bottle plays a flavour tone at the moment you drink. Your brain mixes what it hears with what it tastes.

  1. 1
    Fill it with plain waterStill or sparkling. Nothing else goes in.
  2. 2
    Pick your flavourChoose it in the Aquavida app or on the lid. The display shows your flavour and mode.
  3. 3
    Take a sipWhen you tilt the bottle, the speaker plays the tone for that flavour and the light ring glows in its colour.
  4. 4
    Taste the differenceHigh, bright tones bring out fresh and sweet notes. Low tones bring out deeper, bitter ones.

The idea behind it

Sound is part of how we taste

Researchers call it sonic seasoning: what you hear while you eat or drink changes how you rate the taste.

High pitch, sweet and sourIn tests at the University of Oxford, people matched high-pitched sounds with sweet and sour tastes.
Low pitch, bitterLow-pitched notes were matched with bitter and umami tastes.
About 10% differenceThe same toffee was rated about 10% sweeter with high notes and about 10% more bitter with low notes.

To be clear: sound does not put flavour into water. It shifts how flavour is perceived. Aquavida is a concept that takes this effect and builds a product around it.

Read more: Scientific American, "The Sound (And Taste) Of Music" · Gastropod, "Sonic Seasoning"
